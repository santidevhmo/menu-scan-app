## Strengths

- Category leader: 54,084 ratings in 13 months (~4,160/mo) — 20-500x its nearest lookalikes
- Covers the objection in its own copy: "Works even if the menu has no calories or macros listed"
- Broadest feature surface of any app reviewed: scan, database, chat, tweaks, alcohol options
- Claims global coverage: "meal suggestions from restaurants around the world"

## Weaknesses

- Hard paywall stated in its own listing: only "a limited selection of meals" before subscribing
- Most-reported complaint is feeling misled by the subscription after a long onboarding (secondary source)
- Reported inaccurate calorie counts (secondary source)
- English only
- Seven IAP price points suggests unsettled pricing

## Implication for Menu Scan

**The app to beat, and the reason to be careful.** It already does what Menu Scan does. Its weakness is the funnel and the numbers, not the feature list.
