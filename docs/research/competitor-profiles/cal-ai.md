## Strengths

## Weaknesses

- Does not read menus - cannot help before you order, which is the entire job Menu Scan does
- Requires the food to be in front of you: no decision support, only logging

## Implication for Menu Scan

**The design reference, and the biggest long-term threat.** Adding menu scanning is a feature release for them, not a pivot. Menu Scan's defence is being better at the menu job before they bother.
